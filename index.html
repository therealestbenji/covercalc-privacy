Privacy Policy — CoverCalc

Last updated: September 17, 2026

CoverCalc ("the app") is a trade calculator app for paint, lumber, fabric, tile, and drywall estimates. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

CoverCalc does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. There are no servers operated by us — the app has no backend.

Data stored on your device only:

Calculation history and your unit-system preference are stored locally on your device using standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Purchases:

The one-time "Unlock All" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, our purchase management provider. To process your purchase and verify your entitlement, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous user ID and standard purchase/transaction data from Google Play (such as product ID, purchase token, and transaction date). This data is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identifier by us. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for how they handle this data: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data we do not collect

CoverCalc does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. The app works fully offline except for the purchase/restore flow described above.

Children's privacy

CoverCalc is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If CoverCalc's data practices change (for example, if crash reporting or analytics are added in a future update), this policy will be updated accordingly before that update is released.
