Data we collect

CoverCalc does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. There are no servers operated by us — the app has no backend.

Data stored on your device only:

Calculation history and your unit-system preference are stored locally on your device using standard leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Purchases:

The one-time "Unlock All" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, our purchase management provider. To process your purchase and verify your entitlement, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous user ID and standard purchase/transaction data from Google Play (such as product ID, purchase token, and transa linked to your name, email, or any other personal identifier by us. See RevenueCat's own privacy policyfor how they handle this data: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data we do not collect

CoverCalc does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personoffline except for the purchase/restore flow described above.

Your rights under GDPR (DSGVO)

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access, rectify, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten," Art. 17 GDPR).

In practice, CoverCalc holds almost no personal data itself — your calculation history and settings live only on your device, and we cannot access or delete those remotely (uninstalling the app removes them). The only data connected to you indirectly is the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat/Google Play for your "Unlock All" purchase. To request erasure or have questions about this data, contact us at the email below; we will forward the request to RevenueCat where legally possible. Note that revenue-relevant transaction records may need to be retainerman tax law (§147 AO), even after an erasure request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Children's privacy

CoverCalc is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If CoverCalc's data practices change (for example, if crash reporting or analytics are added in a future update), this policy will be updated accordingly before that update is released.
